## 🌱 My Development Philosophy

**Accessibility is not an afterthought.** I aim to consider different user needs throughout development, including keyboard navigation, screen readers, and permanent, temporary, or situational disabilities.

**Testing should provide confidence, not just numbers.** I believe in meaningful tests, understanding their limitations, and validating real user experiences.

**Documentation matters.** Clear documentation, understandable code, and well-structured Git history are essential parts of maintainable software.

**Continuous learning is part of the job.** I enjoy questioning assumptions, exploring alternative solutions, and understanding the reasoning behind technical decisions.
